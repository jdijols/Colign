## About the Company

ST6 partners with top-tier private equity firms (Insight Partners, TA Associates, Hg, Genstar) to build category-leading enterprise software companies, rapidly scaling them from $100M to $500M+. We've built six platform companies to date with multi-billion-dollar outcomes. Fundamentally, we're builders. We embed inside portfolio companies and aggressively transform how these businesses run. Increasingly, that means building building custom software and systems where humans and AI work in tandem; reimagining entire workflows with AI at their core, not bolting it on as an afterthought.
