colign design system — preview

Aligned weeks. Visible strategy.

A monochrome design system for a tool whose product story is structural alignment. Energy comes from confident type, layered depth without shadows, generous whitespace, and eased motion. No accent color is introduced — the discipline is the brand.

Tokens · 01

Color

Monochrome scale + semantic-only color. Semantic shades are tuned away from Tailwind defaults — quieter, more confident.
