v2 · minimal-first

The floor: what does the plan page look like with only what's essential?

All three options remove the alignment instrument, week pills, week/year subscript, rally-cry metadata, chess tags, hour estimates, and tech abbreviations (DO·1 / O2). Priority is now High / Medium / Low with a small colored dot. Week navigation is just ← Week of June 1 →. Checkbox-style done state. Consumer-friendly, Notion-leaning tone.

A Pure typographic cascade

No boxes, no rules, no tints. Hierarchy from type and indentation alone.

Reads like a Notion page. Rally Cry is the chapter title. Objectives are subheads. Outcomes are quiet headings. Commits are a checklist underneath. The only color is the priority dot on commits. Lightest possible visual weight.

B Left-rule cascade

Same cascade, but Objectives anchor a heavy left rule and Outcomes a lighter one. Containment visible without boxes.

Adds just enough structure to make the parent-child relationship obvious at a glance. The rule weight differs by level (2px for Objective, 1px for Outcome), so eye knows where to start. Slightly more structured than A, still no backgrounds.

C Tinted Outcome blocks

Objectives get an underlined heading. Outcomes get a single tint level holding their commits. One step of background contrast — no nested boxes.

The hierarchy of focus the user asked for: page → Objective break → Outcome block. Commits sit inside the Outcome tint as clean rows. Most "structured" of these three, still nowhere near v1's card-heavy treatment.
